Global Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026 | Document ID: ST-PRIV-2026-V3

At SPICEN TECHNOLOGIES, safeguarding personal data, corporate telemetry, and financial information is a fundamental operational requirement. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and shield data across our websites, client web applications, calculation engines, and software downloads.

1. Information We Collect

We restrict data collection exclusively to information required for legitimate business operations, system performance auditing, and direct client support:

  • Inquiry & Audit Data: Information submitted via website inquiry forms (such as name, phone number, corporate email address, service requested, and custom project parameters) is captured and directed securely into dedicated SPICEN TECHNOLOGIES database sheets.
  • Financial Engine Inputs: Data processed through client applications (such as loan principal amounts, First Monthly Dates [FMD], and interest rates) is calculated transiently or securely stored in isolated Google Sheets bridges configured specifically for the respective client firm.
  • Local Utility Data: Standalone applications like Clutter Wizard, Clipboard Bridge, and Ghost Shelf operate locally on the user's client machine. Local preferences are retained in secure client-side storage or encrypted local vaults and are never transmitted to external telemetry servers.
  • Technical System Logs: Standard web server connection data including IP addresses, user-agent details, timestamp strings, and referral URLs for security auditing and DDoS mitigation.

2. How We Use Collected Data

SPICEN TECHNOLOGIES enforces strict data minimization protocols. Collected information is utilized strictly to:

  • Evaluate client service inquiries, deliver proposals, and provide direct technical support.
  • Execute custom calculations, generate broken period interest schedules, and route transaction audits.
  • Audit system stability, monitor server response times, and prevent unauthorized penetration attempts.
  • Comply with statutory tax, financial auditing, and regulatory reporting obligations under Indian corporate law.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Boundaries

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize client or user data under any circumstances. Data sharing is strictly confined to the following boundaries:

  • SPICEN GROUP Subsidiaries: Information may be shared internally within parent entity SPICEN GROUP solely for consolidated financial auditing or legal compliance.
  • Infrastructure Integration Partners: Secure API calls executed via trusted service infrastructure (e.g., Google Apps Script, Netlify endpoints, or authorized SMS/WhatsApp dispatch gateways) operate under encrypted TLS protocols under contractually bound privacy constraints.
  • Statutory Disclosures: We disclose data only when explicitly compelled by legal court orders, law enforcement warrants, or statutory regulatory mandates under Indian jurisprudence.

4. Data Retention & Client Storage Rights

Client project logs and form inquiry records are retained within secure enterprise storage repositories for as long as necessary to fulfill active contractual engagements or satisfy tax audit requirements. Clients retain the absolute right to request data audit reports or demand complete deletion of contact data from our active form repositories by submitting a formal request to our privacy team.

5. Security of Client Financial Data

Fintech calculations and financial data schedules managed for client applications are processed in isolated execution environments. Access to client Google Sheet data bridges is restricted using zero-trust API credentialing, preventing unauthorized cross-tenant data leaks.

6. Privacy Policy Amendments

SPICEN TECHNOLOGIES updates this policy periodically to reflect evolving technology standards and legislative requirements. Modifications take immediate effect upon posting to this URL.
