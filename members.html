Pioneer & Founder

Navneet Garg

Founder & Chairman, SPICEN GROUP | Sole Proprietor, SPICEN CAPITAL

Navneet Garg is the legal owner and Sole Proprietor of the trade name SPICEN CAPITAL. All corporate commercial trading, credit distributions, and financial transactions under the finance division are officially executed under his registered trade name.

Navneet Garg is the visionary pioneer behind the SPICEN identity. He originally conceptualized the thought. Over time, his entrepreneurial vision laid the groundwork for what evolved into the present-day SPICEN GROUP.

Holding a formal degree in Criminal Law, he possesses deep expertise in legal frameworks and corporate governance. Rather than actively practicing law in courtroom litigation, he channels his strategic legal acumen into the finance and corporate sector. He currently serves as a senior Operations Manager at a Finance LTD Company, where he manages loan distributions, micro-finance portfolios, and group loan scheduling. Simultaneously, he acts as Director of SPICEN CAPITAL, guiding credit solutions and financial management operations.

Qualifications Criminal Law Degree (LL.B.)
Key Roles Sole Proprietor (SPICEN CAPITAL), Chairman (SPICEN GROUP)
Primary Expertise Credit Operations, Group Loans & Legal Compliance

Executive & Technical Lead

Nehal Agarwal

Director & CEO, SPICEN TECHNOLOGIES

Nehal Agarwal is the founder, CEO, and primary architect of SPICEN TECHNOLOGIES. Carrying forward the family's entrepreneurial legacy, he transformed the brand into a technology powerhouse specializing in web development, real-time data automation dashboards, and integrated hardware solutions.

He is currently pursuing his B.Tech degree in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) at IIIT Allahabad. Combining academic rigor with hands-on development skills, he bridges software architecture with hardware design—aiming to position SPICEN TECHNOLOGIES at the forefront of semiconductor engineering, microcontrollers, and embedded hardware communication.

Beyond engineering and corporate leadership, Nehal is an avid coder and creative writer with a passion for poetry. His balance of technical precision and artistic perspective shapes the clean visual identity, user interface design, and philosophy of SPICEN TECHNOLOGIES.

For more information you may visit: INFO_NEHAL
