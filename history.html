The SPICEN Legacy

How a visionary dream evolved into a multi-sector corporate group.

A Shared Vision Across Generations

The name SPICEN originated as a entrepreneurial dream pioneered by Navneet Garg. Grounded in excellence, reliability, and client trust, the brand laid a strong foundation in corporate service delivery.

Driven by a desire to preserve and expand this legacy into modern horizons, the brand was re-architected into SPICEN GROUP—a parent conglomerate housing distinct business divisions. Under this group, Nehal Agarwal founded SPICEN TECHNOLOGIES, channeling the spirit of the original name into high-tech software engineering, custom platforms, and electronics innovation.
